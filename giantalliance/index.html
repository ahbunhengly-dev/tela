Our story

From three people in a 20 m² office to a team of 80, each new business started because our clients needed it.

  1. 2016

    GIANTFOCUS is founded

    Online marketing and advertising for companies in Cambodia. It grows into one of the leading local digital marketing agencies.

  2. 2017

    Officially registered

    A legal business, three people and a small office of about 20 m². 10 members by the end of the year.

  3. 2018

    GIANTBROTHER starts

    A move to a new office and the start of our tax and accounting firm. 18 members.

  4. 2019

    CLOSO and our own office

    With a growing team we buy our own office and launch CLOSO business software. 28 members.

  5. 2020

    The MAQSU team begins

    Our software succeeds, so we start building MAQSU, our own business platform. 35 members.

  6. 2021

    Stronger through Covid-19

    The pandemic hits hard, but the business stays strong. 38 members.

  7. 2022

    A bigger home

    We decide to buy a bigger office to prepare for future growth. 42 members.

  8. 2024

    ClickHR launches

    A stronger year after the 2023 economic slowdown, and the start of our HR software. 60 members.

  9. 2025

    A team of 80

    GiantAlliance grows to 80 team members across its businesses.

  10. 2026

    Countory and what’s next

    Our stock counting business starts, alongside new AI features in MAQSU and ClickHR.
